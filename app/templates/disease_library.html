Pomegranate Disease Library

Learn to identify common diseases affecting pomegranate crops.

Bacterial Blight

Pathogen: Xanthomonas axonopodis pv. punicae

Symptoms: Water-soaked, dark, irregular spots on leaves and fruits. Turns into black oily lesions. Severe infections lead to deep cracking of the fruit.

Treatment: Streptomycin sulphate or Copper oxychloride.

Anthracnose

Pathogen: Colletotrichum gloeosporioides

Symptoms: Small, regular or irregular, black sunken spots on the fruit. Coalesce and cause significant rotting.

Treatment: Mancozeb, Propiconazole, or Carbendazim.

Fruit Rot

Pathogen: Fungal (Alternaria, Aspergillus)

Symptoms: Soft, watery rot, starting from the calyx or a wound. Fuzzy fungal growth (white, gray, or black) often appears.

Treatment: Systemic fungicides, prevent insect damage.

Healthy

Status: No visible disease symptoms.

Symptoms: Firm skin, natural color, no lesions or rot.

Treatment: Maintain proper irrigation and monitoring.
